# Speaker Notes

**Sesi:** Memanfaatkan Agentic AI untuk Meningkatkan Produktiviti Harian  
**Penceramah:** PM Dr. Mohd Murtadha Mohamad  
**Tarikh:** 21 Julai 2026 (Selasa)  
**Masa:** 10.00 pagi–12.00 tengah hari

## Agenda latihan 2 jam

- Slide 1 and opening activity: 10 minutes
- Slides 2–3, concepts and examples: 15 minutes
- Slide 4, live demonstration: 15 minutes
- Hands-on 1 using the template: 25 minutes
- Break and sharing: 10 minutes
- Slide 5 and Hands-on 2 in Gemini Gems: 25 minutes
- Testing, reflection, and human approval: 12 minutes
- Slide 6, responsible-use checklist and closing: 8 minutes

## Slide 1 — UTM Agentic AI Training

Welcome participants and frame the hour around practical daily productivity. Emphasise that useful agents begin with narrow, observable tasks.

## Slide 2 — AI assistant vs agentic AI

Contrast a reusable instruction-following assistant with an agentic workflow that observes state, chooses actions, uses tools, checks results, and escalates.

## Slide 3 — Workplace examples

Use lecturer and administrative examples. Ask participants which steps require judgment, tool access, evidence, or human approval.

## Slide 4 — From vague request to workflow

Live-build the workflow from goal to inputs, persistent instructions, output, tests, and approval. Then introduce the one-page template for Hands-on 1.

## Slide 5 — Build and test in Gemini Gems

Show how to create a Gem in the web app, add persistent instructions, save, and test. Use an expected case, an edge case, and an unsafe or ambiguous case.

## Slide 6 — Responsible deployment

Close with privacy, permissions, traceability, human oversight, monitoring, and a bounded pilot. Invite teams to state one next step and one unresolved risk.
